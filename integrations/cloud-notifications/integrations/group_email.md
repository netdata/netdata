## Setup


Group Email sends Space-wide alert notifications to a shared group or mailing-list email address, independently of each member's personal email notification settings.

### Prerequisites

- A Netdata Cloud account
- Access to the Space as an **Admin**
- The Space needs to be on a paid plan
- Access to the mailbox of the group or mailing-list address, in order to retrieve the verification token

### Netdata Configuration

1. Click on the **Space settings** cog (located above your profile icon)
2. Click on the **Alerts & Notifications** tab
3. Click on the **+ Add configuration** button
4. Add the Group Email Integration
5. A modal will be presented to you to enter the required details to enable the integration:
    - **Notification settings**
      - Configuration name (optional): A name for your configuration to easily refer to it
      - Rooms: A list of Rooms for which you want to be notified
      - Notifications: The notification types you want to receive
    - **Integration configuration**
      - Email address: The group or mailing-list address that will receive the notifications
    - **Verification**
      - Token: The token from the latest **Test notification** email received on the group email address.
        - Click on the **Test** button to send a test email to the address you entered. The verification token is embedded in that email; copy it back into the modal to verify the address.
